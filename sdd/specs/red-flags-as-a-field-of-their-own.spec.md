# Spec: Red flags as a field of their own

**Status:** Draft
**Description:** Red flags as a field of their own

_Edit via `scripts/spec.py`, never by hand._

## Goals
- **G-1** Warning signs are a distinct field, rendered apart from ordinary guidance and reachable during a running session
- **G-2** A warning sign carries the action, not only the sign: stop and call, not see a doctor if

## Prior Art
- **PA-1** A sports physiotherapist reviewing the guidance prototype on 2026-09-09 observed the red flags rendering as see a doctor if, with no instruction to stop, three lines below do not stop for moderate pain and above a metronome still beeping, and called that ordering worse than either sentence alone
- **PA-2** The same reviewer observed that pain up to 5/10 survived every treatment while reduce the load ten percent after a flare survived none, and that a threshold whose action is missing is not a safety rule but trivia
- **PA-3** A JAMIA scoping review found that where clinical information suggests danger the appropriate response must be clear and explicit, and that only 28 of 121 apps handling high-risk data responded appropriately (https://academic.oup.com/jamia/article/27/2/330/5585394)
- **PA-4** Deferred out of guidance-a-program-can-carry on 2026-09-09 so it is decided on its own evidence rather than as a rider on the guidance fields
